Privacy Policy for ThyroCheck

Effective Date: May 1, 2026

Last Updated: May 1, 2026

IMPORTANT MEDICAL DISCLAIMER: ThyroCheck is designed solely for informational, educational, and preliminary risk-screening purposes. It does NOT diagnose clinical thyroid conditions or replace professional medical consultation, diagnostic blood tests (e.g., TSH, Free T3/T4), or advice from a qualified healthcare provider.

1. Overview

ThyroCheck ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is handled when you use the ThyroCheck mobile application.

2. Zero Personal Data Collection & Local Processing

ThyroCheck is engineered with a strict Privacy-First, On-Device Architecture:

  • No Data Collection: We do NOT collect, transmit, store on external servers, or sell any personal data, health assessments, demographic inputs, or usage metrics.
  • Local Storage Only: All assessment inputs (age, gender, symptom selections, and saved assessment records) are stored locally on your device using an encrypted, isolated Room SQLite database.
  • No Account Creation: No user registration, login, email address, or user accounts are required to use the app.

3. Internet Access & External Services

ThyroCheck operates completely offline for core risk assessment functionality. The app does not transmit health data to any cloud service or third-party server.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

Because no personal data is collected or transmitted, we do not share, sell, trade, or transfer any user data to third parties, analytics providers, or advertisers.

5. Children's Privacy

ThyroCheck is intended for adult users aged 18 and older seeking preliminary thyroid health awareness. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

All saved assessment records remain exclusively on your device. You can delete individual records or clear your entire history at any time directly within the app's History tab. Uninstalling the app will permanently delete all locally saved records.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be reflected on this page with an updated Effective Date.
